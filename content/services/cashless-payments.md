Card readers are standard on everything we place. Most sites now run the majority of their sales cashless, and a machine that only takes coins quietly loses a third of its traffic.

Every unit has a drop sensor. If the product does not fall, the sale does not complete and the refund is automatic — so nobody on your floor has to come find you about a lost dollar.
