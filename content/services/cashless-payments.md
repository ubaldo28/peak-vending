Card readers come as standard on everything we place. Most sites now take the bulk of their sales contactless, and a machine that only swallows coins quietly loses a third of its trade.

Every unit has a drop sensor. If the item does not fall, the sale does not complete and the refund happens by itself — so nobody has to come and find you about a lost pound.
