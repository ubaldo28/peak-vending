Once a site gets big enough, a machine stops being the right answer — the range is too narrow and the queue is too slow at shift change.

A micro market is an open shelf-and-chiller setup with a self-checkout kiosk. Far more range, fresh items included, and folk can pick something up and read the back of it. It needs a secure room and the headcount to justify it, which we work out when we come round.
