Once a site gets big enough, a machine stops being the right answer — the range is too narrow and the queue is too slow at shift change.

A micro market is an open shelf-and-cooler setup with a self-checkout kiosk. Far more range, including fresh items, and people can pick things up and read the label. It needs a secure room and enough headcount to justify it, which we work out at the survey.
