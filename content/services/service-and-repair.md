There is a sticker on every machine with a phone number, and it rings us. Not a manufacturer support line, not a ticket queue, not a call centre in another time zone.

The usual faults — a jam, a dead card reader, a coin mech that has stopped accepting — get fixed on the visit, because the parts are already on the truck.
