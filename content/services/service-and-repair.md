There is a sticker on every machine with a phone number on it, and it rings us. Not a manufacturer's helpline, not a ticket queue, not a call centre in another time zone.

The usual faults — a jam, a dead card reader, a coin mech that has stopped taking — get sorted on the visit, because the parts are already on the van.
