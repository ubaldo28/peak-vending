Filling is where most vending arrangements fall apart. A machine gets loaded once with whatever was on the van, and six months later it is still half full of the same thing nobody wanted.

We set the range with you, then let the sales figures correct it. If a slot is not shifting after a fair go, it comes out. You should not have to ask twice for that.
