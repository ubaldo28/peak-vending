Stocking is where most vending relationships fall apart. A machine gets filled once with whatever was on the truck, and three months later it is still half full of the same thing nobody wanted.

We set the lineup with you, then let the sales data correct it. If a slot is not moving after a fair run, it comes out. You should never have to ask twice for that.
